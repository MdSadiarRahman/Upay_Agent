UpayPulse AI Knowledge Base
===========================

# About UpayPulse AI
UpayPulse AI is an AI-powered financial intelligence platform connecting customers, merchants, and agents.

# Responsible AI Rules
- AI provides financial insights only.
- AI does not approve or reject loans.
- AI cannot execute transactions autonomously without user confirmation.

# Privacy Knowledge
- Data Permission: Users have full control over their data. AI only uses data that the user has explicitly consented to share.
- Security: All personal data is anonymized before AI processing.
- User Control: Users can revoke AI data access at any time through the Privacy Center.

# Features
- Customer Dashboard: Track expenses, manage smart bills, and receive AI financial insights.
- Merchant Dashboard: Monitor growth, customer retention, and sales intelligence.
- Agent Dashboard: Track liquidity and manage cash flow.
- AI Financial Assistant: A chatbot for answering financial questions and providing insights based on secure data.
